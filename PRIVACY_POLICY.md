# Reticle — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. Overview

Reticle ("the App") is a document scanner application developed by Isaac. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 2. What We Collect

### Camera Access
The App requests camera permission solely to capture document images for scanning. Camera access is used only when you actively scan a document.

### Document Data
All scanned documents, images, and OCR text are stored **locally on your device**. Document data is never uploaded to any server or cloud service.

### Advertising Data (Free Tier)
The free version displays a single banner advertisement on the library screen, powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:
- Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
- General device information (model, OS version)
- IP address (for ad serving and fraud prevention)

This data collection is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Pro Tier
Reticle Pro removes all advertisements and their associated data collection. Purchase transactions are handled by Google Play and are subject to [Google Play's Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

## 3. What We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** require an account or registration
- We do **not** collect your name, email, or contact information
- We do **not** upload your documents or scanned images
- We do **not** track your location
- We do **not** share any data with third parties (aside from AdMob in the free tier)
- We do **not** use analytics or tracking SDKs

## 4. On-Device Processing

All document processing — including image capture, filters, perspective correction, and OCR text recognition — is performed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit). No document data leaves your device at any time.

## 5. Data Storage & Deletion

- Documents are stored in the App's private storage on your device
- You can delete any document at any time from within the App
- Uninstalling the App removes all stored data permanently
- The App's Settings screen includes a storage management tool to clear processed images and thumbnails

## 6. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | Required |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| Camera | Capture document images for scanning | Optional (can import from gallery instead) |

No other permissions are requested.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is a general-purpose utility with no age-restricted content.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
